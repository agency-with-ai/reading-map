# Read before checking the wiki

With Claude closed, skim each paper's abstract, introduction, figures, and conclusion. Use the four questions from [Lab 2](https://agencyai.mit.edu/lab2/).

| Question | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Motivation | Why should this problem be solved? |
| Solution | How did the authors solve it, and how did they judge the method? |
| Old idea | How did earlier work approach the problem? |
| Delta | What did this paper do differently? Compare with earlier work to assess the novelty claim. |

Record brief answers in `lab3-note.md`. Mark gaps rather than guessing. Read the passages needed for your project decision closely, including the assumptions, comparisons, and limits. Return to the original papers when the wiki adds a claim or connection your skim did not establish.
