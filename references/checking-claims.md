# Check a claim before using it

Choose one claim that matters to your project choice. Follow its wiki link to the source record and open the original paper at the cited passage.

- Does the passage support the claim, including its qualifiers?
- What inputs, data, metric, baseline, or assumptions does the result depend on?
- Does the paper report this result, or is it an inference by Claude or by you?
- For a connection between papers, what does each passage contribute?
- Which conditions match your project, and which remain untested?

Record the passage location, your check, and whether the claim is supported, corrected, or unresolved in `lab3-note.md`. A paper's use of a method establishes precedent under its conditions. It does not establish that the method will work for your project.
