# Read the writing-project example

[Write like a professor (2026)](../spec-example.md) asks whether readers can distinguish a professor's writing from generated prose. Read its writing brief and first build together. The brief supplies something concrete to try, and the checks say what would make a draft unacceptable.

The project needs evidence about how to represent the author's style and how to compare real and generated passages. Reading papers can change either choice.

For your own spec, ask another reader to judge one possible result before you explain your intent. If they cannot, identify the missing example or requirement. If the decision needs research, name what you need to learn.

For further reading, see 6.102's [software specifications (2026)](https://web.mit.edu/6.102/www/sp26/classes/04-specifications/) and [designing specifications (2026)](https://web.mit.edu/6.102/www/sp26/classes/05-designing-specs/).
